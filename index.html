The Veteran's Playbook: Root Cause Coaching

Numbers on a dashboard are just symptoms. The root cause of metric drag is always either a behavioral block or a deviation from the Tech Step Document. When you sit down with your outliers, don't just point at the scoreboard—diagnose the specific step they are skipping or the mindset holding them back. Use these talking points during your ride-alongs and 1-on-1s.

⏱️

PPH (Productivity)

Step-by-Step Execution

Audit the Pre-Arrival: Are they completing the inventory and tool check before knocking on the door? Most time is bled walking back to the van. Enforce the "One Trip" diagnostic setup strictly as outlined in the initial site-survey phase.

Behavioral Coaching

Watch for "Time Blindness": Do they chat too much after the work is done? Coach them on polite transition phrasing. "I'm so glad we got this working for you today! I need to wrap up my paperwork to get to my next customer, but do you have any final questions on the remote?"

💵

$/WO (Revenue)

Step-by-Step Execution

Audit the Discovery Phase: Are they skipping the lifestyle questionnaire? The Tech Step Document requires evaluating the home for audio, Wi-Fi dead zones, and surge protection *before* starting the main install.

Behavioral Coaching

Overcoming "Wallet Assuming": Underperformers often pre-judge what a customer can afford and decide not to pitch. Coach them that our job is to offer solutions, not manage the customer's budget. Roleplay the transition from discovery to recommendation.

✅

DCR (Completion)

Step-by-Step Execution

Audit the Troubleshooting Tree: Are they utilizing the required support channels (internal chat/Tier 2) before calling a job a wash? Ensure they are exhausting the documented diagnostic tree before canceling a work order.

Behavioral Coaching

Building Technical Confidence: Low DCR often stems from a tech getting overwhelmed by complex setups (e.g., custom home theaters, complex wiring). Pair them with a senior tech for a complex job ride-along to build their confidence when the "easy fix" isn't available.

🔄

R12% (Quality / Rollbacks)

Step-by-Step Execution

Audit the Wrap-Up & QC: High R12 is almost always a failure to verify. Are they running the final signal tests? Are they physically showing the customer how to use the equipment and making them perform the action themselves before leaving?

Behavioral Coaching

The "Rush to Finish" Mindset: R12 spikes when techs feel behind schedule and cut corners. Coach them that spending an extra 5 minutes on customer education today prevents a 2-hour rollback tomorrow. Slow down to speed up.
